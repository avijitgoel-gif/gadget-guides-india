## How to choose a wireless mouse under ₹1,000

### USB receiver, Bluetooth, or both

A USB receiver mouse works the moment you plug it in, including on older PCs and in BIOS screens. Bluetooth keeps ports free and suits Macs, iPads and slim laptops with only USB-C. Dual mode mice like the Portronics Toad One and Toad 8 give you both and let you switch between devices.

### AA battery or rechargeable

AA models such as the Logitech M221 and B170 claim 12 to 18 months on a battery, and you swap a cell in seconds. Rechargeable models avoid buying batteries but need charging every few weeks, depending on use and RGB lighting. If you also need a charger for your phone, see our [USB-C charger guide]({{ "/best-usb-c-charger-for-iphone-under-1500-in-india/" | relative_url }}).

### Silent clicks

Silent buttons are listed on the Logitech M221 and M240, and the Portronics Toad 8 and ZEBRONICS Blanc Slim also claim quiet clicking. This matters most in shared offices, hostels and homes with sleeping kids.

### DPI

Most office mice ship at around 1000 DPI, which is fine for normal screens. Higher or adjustable DPI, as on the Toad 7 (up to 3200 DPI, ₹449), moves the pointer further for each inch of hand movement, which helps on large or high resolution monitors.

### Ratings count matters more than the star score

A 4.3 from 79,308 buyers (Logitech B170) is a stronger signal than a 4.0 from 27 buyers (Acer Flow Silent at ₹299). Read recent one-star reviews for double clicking and scroll wheel complaints, the two most common mouse failures.

### What is the best wireless mouse under ₹1,000 in India?
For most people, the Logitech M221 at ₹725 is the best pick, with silent buttons, an 18 month battery claim and 4.3 stars from over 43,000 ratings. On a tighter budget, the Logitech B170 at ₹445 is the most rated option.

### Is a Bluetooth mouse better than a USB receiver mouse?
Not better, just different. Bluetooth frees up USB ports and suits Macs and tablets. A USB receiver connects instantly and works on any PC with a USB-A port. Dual mode mice offer both.

### Which wireless mouse under ₹1,000 works with a MacBook?
The Logitech M240 Silent and M196 are Bluetooth mice listed as compatible with macOS, so they need no receiver. Dual mode Portronics and Arctic Fox mice also work over Bluetooth.
