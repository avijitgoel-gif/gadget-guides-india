## How to choose a smart plug in India

### 10A or 16A

Indian homes use two common socket sizes. The smaller three-pin socket powers TVs, chargers, lamps and routers. The larger 16A socket is usually fitted for geysers, ACs, microwaves and heaters. Match the plug to both the socket and the appliance: a 16A plug for a 2,000W room heater, a 10A plug for a TV. If you are buying a heater this winter, our sister site's [room heater guide](https://avijitgoel-gif.github.io/home-kitchen-india/best-room-heater-under-2000-in-india/) and [immersion rod guide](https://avijitgoel-gif.github.io/home-kitchen-india/best-immersion-rod-under-1000-in-india/) list wattages so you can check the load.

### Energy monitoring

Most plugs here show live power use in the app. That turns a guess into a number. A 2,000W appliance used for one hour consumes 2 kWh, so multiply by your electricity tariff per unit to see the cost. Seeing this daily is what makes people cut a geyser from 45 minutes to 20.

### 2.4GHz Wi-Fi only

The Wipro manual states its plug does not support 5GHz Wi-Fi, and this is true of most budget smart plugs. If your router has one combined network name for both bands, setup can fail. Most routers let you split the bands or enable a separate 2.4GHz network for setup.

### Voice assistants and apps

Every plug in the table lists Alexa and Google Assistant support except the Lifelong, which mentions voice control without naming the assistants in its title. Each brand uses its own app, so stick to one brand if you plan to buy several plugs.

### Power cuts

The Wipro manual says its plug returns to its previous state after a power failure, but schedules running during the cut may behave differently. Check this setting in the app for any appliance that should stay off after power returns. ## FAQ

### What is the best smart plug under ₹1,000 in India?
For most homes, the Wipro 16A Wi-Fi Smart Plug at ₹869 is the safest pick, with energy monitoring, Alexa and Google support and 4.2 stars from over 19,000 ratings. The Tapo P110 at ₹848 has the higher star rating at 4.4.

### Can a smart plug run a geyser or AC?
Yes, if it is a 16A plug in a 16A socket and the appliance stays within that rating. The Wipro 16A, Tapo P110, Qubo 16A, HomeMate 16A and AGARO Regency all list heavy appliances such as geysers or ACs. Make sure the plug sits firmly in the socket.

### Do smart plugs work on 5GHz Wi-Fi?
Most budget smart plugs, including the Wipro DSP1160, work only on 2.4GHz Wi-Fi. Enable a 2.4GHz network on your router for setup.

### Does a smart plug save electricity?
A smart plug uses a small amount of power itself, but energy monitoring and schedules help you cut the running time of heavy appliances like geysers and heaters, which is where the savings come from.

### Is a 10A smart plug enough?
A 10A plug is enough for TVs, set top boxes, routers, lamps and phone or laptop chargers. Use a 16A plug for geysers, ACs, microwaves, room heaters and water pumps.
