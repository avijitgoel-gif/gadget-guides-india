## The big watt number is a sum, not a speed

Look at the Ambrane C51. The box says 51W. The listing explains where that comes from: a 33W USB-C port plus an 18W USB-A port. Plug in one phone and the most it can ever get is 33W. The AGARO Pulse works the same way.

The Portronics Car Power 30 is a sharper example. Its title says "30W Type-C PD and 30W USB", which reads like 60W. The first bullet on the same page says the charger has "a max output of 30 watts" across its dual ports, and the spec table lists 30 watts. So treat it as a 30W charger that shares that power when both ports are busy.

The Duracell 36W lists its real output in plain numbers: Quick Charge 3.0 at 3.6 to 6.5V 3A, 6.5 to 9V 2A, or 9 to 12V 1.5A per port. The top step works out to 18W. Two ports at 18W each gives you the 36W on the box.

So when you compare car chargers, ignore the headline and read the per-port line. For a single phone, 20W to 33W on one USB-C port is the number that matters.

## PD and PPS: the part that decides fast charging

A charger can have enough watts and still charge your phone slowly if it does not support the right protocol.

### iPhone needs USB-C Power Delivery

Apple's support page says that to fast charge an iPhone 15 or later, or an earlier model with a USB-C to Lightning cable, you need an Apple 18W or greater USB-C adapter or "a comparable third-party USB-C power adapter that supports USB-PD". The page also says iPhone 17e and iPhone Air reach 50 percent in about 30 minutes with a 20W or higher adapter, while iPhone 17 and 17 Pro models need 40W or more for their quoted speed.

That rules out the Duracell 36W for iPhone fast charging, since it only has USB-A Quick Charge ports. Every other charger here has a USB-C PD port of 20W or more, which covers iPhone 15, iPhone 16, iPhone 17e and iPhone Air. None of these chargers reaches the 40W Apple quotes for iPhone 17 fast charging, though they will still charge it at a decent clip.

### Samsung Super Fast Charging needs PPS

Samsung's own support page puts it bluntly: to use Super fast charging, check whether the charger supports USB PD PPS, because "even if a charger has a high maximum output, Super fast charging may not be available if the charger does not support PPS." Samsung's own 25W charger lists PD 3.0 PPS up to 25W.

Only two chargers in our list name PPS on their Amazon.in page: the boAt 55W (PD, QC and PPS) and the Portronics Car Power 1C (its 33W Type-C cable is described as PPS). If you drive with a Galaxy phone, those are the two to pick. The others say PD and may well work, but we would not assume Super Fast Charging without PPS on the spec sheet.

## Buying guide: what to check before you order

1. **Per-port USB-C output.** Look for 20W or more on one USB-C port for a modern phone. Ignore the total on the box.
2. **PD for iPhone, PPS for Samsung.** Check the listing text, not just the title. If PPS is not mentioned, assume it is not there.
3. **How many devices.** Two people charging at once need a charger with two strong ports, or the power gets shared.
4. **Built-in cable or not.** A coiled or retractable cable saves you carrying one, but if it breaks, the whole charger is done. A plain two-port charger with your own good cable lasts longer.
5. **Input voltage.** Most cars use 12V. If you drive a 24V vehicle, pick one that lists 12 to 24V input, like the Ambrane C51 or Duracell.
6. **Unplug when parked if your socket stays live.** In many cars the 12V socket switches off with the ignition. If yours does not, a charger with an LED left plugged in for days is a small but needless drain on the battery.

### Which is the best car charger under Rs 1,000 in India?

### Does a 51W car charger charge my phone at 51W?
No. The 51W figure on chargers like the Ambrane C51 and AGARO Pulse is 33W from the USB-C port plus 18W from the USB-A port. A single phone gets at most 33W from the USB-C port, and only if the phone and charger agree on a fast charging protocol.

### Which car charger fast charges an iPhone?
Apple says iPhone 15 and later need an 18W or greater USB-C adapter that supports USB PD to fast charge. Any charger here with a USB-C PD port of 20W or more works for iPhone 15, iPhone 16, iPhone 17e and iPhone Air. The Duracell 36W has only USB-A ports, so it will not fast charge an iPhone.

### Which car charger supports Samsung Super Fast Charging?
Samsung says Super fast charging needs a charger that supports USB PD PPS. Among the chargers we checked, the boAt 55W and the Portronics Car Power 1C name PPS on their Amazon.in listings, so they are the safest picks for Galaxy phones.

### Can a car charger drain my car battery?
Only if your car's 12V socket stays live with the ignition off. In many cars it switches off with the ignition. If yours stays on, unplug the charger when you park for a few days.
