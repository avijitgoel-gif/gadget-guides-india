## What the extra ₹280 buys

**Quiet clicks.** This is the main reason to pick the M221. The listing claims 90% less click sound with the same click feel. In a shared office, a hostel room or a home where someone is asleep, you will notice it every day.

**A longer battery claim.** Logitech claims 18 months for the M221 against 12 months for the B170. Both figures depend on how much you use the mouse, but the M221 also lists auto sleep.

**A place to store the receiver.** The M221 listing says the receiver sits in a small slot next to the battery compartment, which helps if you carry the mouse in a laptop bag.
