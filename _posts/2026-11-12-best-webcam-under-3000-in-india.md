## 720p or 1080p: what your calling app actually sends

- **Google Meet:** Google's help page says 1080p sending is only available on certain paid plans, such as Workspace Business Standard and above, Workspace Individual, and Google One plans with 2 TB or more. A normal free Gmail account can choose up to 720p. Google also notes 1080p is not supported in Firefox or Safari.
- **Zoom:** Zoom's help page says Standard HD (720p) in group meetings needs a paid Pro, Business, Education or Enterprise account. Full HD (1080p) needs a Business, Education or Enterprise account (or specific Pro add-ons), has to be enabled by Zoom Support, needs a fast CPU, and does not work with virtual backgrounds switched on.

So if you mostly use free Zoom or Meet, a 1080p webcam will not make you look 1080p to the other side. 1080p still helps if you record videos locally, stream with OBS, or use a paid Workspace or Zoom plan.

## How to choose a webcam in India

### Field of view

A narrow lens (55° on the C270) frames one face. A wide lens (95° on the Lenovo) fits two people, but also shows more of the room behind you.

### Fixed focus or autofocus

Fixed-focus cameras are sharp at sitting distance and soft when something is held close. Autofocus helps with documents and product demos.

### Light matters more than megapixels

Review summaries for the Lenovo and HP both mention dark or grainy images. Face a window or put a lamp behind your screen, not behind you. That often does more than a pricier camera.

### Mic and headset

In a noisy house, a wired headset beats any webcam mic. Our [wired earphones under ₹500 guide]({{ "/best-wired-earphones-under-500-in-india/" | relative_url }}) and [TWS earbuds under ₹2,000 guide]({{ "/best-tws-earbuds-under-2000-in-india/" | relative_url }}) cover cheap options.

### Ports and privacy

These webcams plug into USB-A, so a USB-C-only laptop needs an adapter. A physical shutter is worth having, and the C270 listing does not mention one.
