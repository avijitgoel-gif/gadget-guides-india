## How to choose a 20000mAh power bank

### 1. Match the wattage to your phone

Most Android phones in India charge at 18W to 33W over a power bank, and iPhones top out around 20W to 27W. A 22.5W power bank is enough for most people. Pay for 33W if your phone supports it, and for 65W only if you also want to charge a laptop.

### 2. Expect about 60% to 65% of the rated capacity

A 20000mAh power bank does not deliver 20000mAh to your phone. Voltage conversion and heat lose about a third. In practice, expect 12000mAh to 13000mAh of real charge, which is roughly 2.5 full charges for a 5000mAh phone.

### 3. Prefer Type-C input

Recharging a 20000mAh power bank over Micro USB can take eight hours or more. Type-C PD input usually cuts that to four to five hours.

### 4. Check flight rules

Indian airlines allow power banks in cabin baggage only, and 20000mAh (about 74Wh) is under the common 100Wh limit. Never pack a power bank in checked luggage. Check your airline's current rules before you fly, because some carriers have restricted using power banks on board.

### 5. Ignore the MRP

Every listing in the table shows an MRP two to five times the selling price. Compare the selling price and the rating count instead.
