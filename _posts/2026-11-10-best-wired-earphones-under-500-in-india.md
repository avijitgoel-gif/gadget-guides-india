## How to choose wired earphones in India

### Check your phone's port first

If your phone has a 3.5mm jack, a 3.5mm pair like the boAt BassHeads 210 also works with laptops, car stereos and most other devices. If your phone only has a USB-C port, you need Type-C earphones or a USB-C to 3.5mm adapter. Apple sells such an adapter and says it lets you connect 3.5mm headphones to a USB-C port.

### Why Type-C earphones sometimes do not work

Audio over a USB-C port is not as simple as a 3.5mm jack. Portronics tells buyers to enable the OTG setting if its Type-C earphones do not connect, and reviews of several Type-C models in this list mention phones they did not work with. Apple's support page notes that devices and cables that are not compliant with the USB-C standard may not work as expected. Test any Type-C pair as soon as it arrives, inside Amazon's replacement window.

### Warranty matters more than driver size

At this price, the most common complaint across all these listings is one side going silent or the mic failing. A 1-year warranty, like boAt's, is worth more than a bigger driver. Note that claims are handled at the brand's service centre, not by Amazon, after the 10-day replacement period.

### Fit and ear tips

Fit is the second biggest complaint. Pairs that ship with several ear tip sizes, like the Philips, give you a better chance of a good seal, which also improves bass.

### Do Type-C earphones work with every phone?
Not always. Portronics advises turning on the OTG setting if its Type-C earphones do not connect, and reviews of several Type-C models mention phones they did not work with. Test them as soon as they arrive.

### Will cheap Type-C earphones work with an iPhone 15 or later?
Some do and some do not, according to buyer reviews. Apple's EarPods (USB-C) are listed by Apple as compatible with USB-C iPhones running iOS 17 or later, so they are the safest choice for an iPhone.

### Are wired earphones better than Bluetooth earbuds for calls and gaming?
Wired earphones never need charging and have no Bluetooth connection delay, which helps in games and video calls. Bluetooth earbuds are more convenient but cost more for similar sound.

### What warranty do budget wired earphones have?
It varies. On Amazon.in, the boAt BassHeads 210 lists a 1-year warranty, while the Portronics Conch Theta C and Philips TAE1159 list 6 months. Warranty claims go to the brand's service centre.
