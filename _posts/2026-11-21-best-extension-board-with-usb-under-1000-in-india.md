## The USB ports are slow, and that is fine if you know it

USB output is the biggest gap between the box and reality. Zebronics lists its Power BAR USB ports at 5V 1A maximum, which is 5 watts. Honeywell says its Type-C and two USB-A ports deliver up to 10W individually and 10W combined. Portronics rates the four ports on the Power Plate 6 at 2.1A total, which at USB's standard 5 volts is about 10.5W shared across all four.

Compare that with what a modern phone wants. Apple's support page on fast charging says iPhone 15 and later need an 18W or greater USB-C Power Delivery adapter to fast charge, and the iPhone 17 needs 40W or higher to reach 50% in about 20 minutes. A 5W or 10W USB port will still charge the phone. It will just take hours.

Treat the USB ports as a convenience for earbuds, a watch or overnight charging. For quick top ups, plug a proper charger into a socket. ## What "2500W" really means on a 6A plug

Most boards in this price range carry the standard Indian 6A three pin plug. Havells puts the honest version of the maths on its own 6A board's listing: 6A at 240V is "up to 1440W". Honeywell rates its board at 6A and 1500W, which is in the same range.

Portronics and GM both rate their boards at 2500W, and Zebronics rates its Power BARs at 2000W and 10A, though the Power BAR 145 spec table lists a Type D plug, the common Indian 6A type. Whatever the board can handle, a 6A wall socket feeding a "2500W" board is still a 6A circuit.

In practice:

- A laptop, monitor, router, chargers and a TV together draw a few hundred watts. Any board here is fine.
- A 2,000W room heater alone draws close to 9A at 230V, more than a 6A socket is rated for. Plug heaters, geysers, irons and induction cooktops straight into a 16A wall socket.
- Do not plug one board into another. A plugs and sockets report published by the Department of Consumer Affairs calls this a fire risk that overloads the extension, and says the current rating marked on a lead should never be exceeded.

A spike guard does not keep a PC running in a power cut. ## What BIS says about universal sockets

IS 1293 is the mandatory Indian standard for plugs and socket-outlets, under a Quality Control Order in force since 1 June 2022. A Bureau of Indian Standards "Information to Industry" note on it (Version IV, 29 May 2023) lists "Multistandard Socket" and "6A, 5pin Universal Socket" among varieties that "are not covered in IS 1293 : 2005 and IS 1293 : 2019 and hence cannot be manufactured or sold". It adds that a cord extension set as a whole is outside IS 1293, though the standard applies to its plug and sockets. A separate standard, IS 18830 : 2024, has since been published for cord extension sets.

Yet almost every USB board on Amazon.in, including models from Havells, Anchor by Panasonic, Goldmedal and Honeywell, uses universal sockets. We found no popular USB board under ₹1,000 with plain Indian 6A sockets. So:

- Check that the plug and cord are ISI or BIS marked. Havells and Honeywell say so in their listings.
- Prefer safety shutters if children are at home. Honeywell lists one.
- Make sure plugs sit firmly. The consumer affairs report says loose connections cause many melted sockets and fires through arcing. If a plug wobbles, use another socket.

## FAQ

### Which is the best extension board with USB under Rs 1,000 in India?
The Portronics Power Plate 6 at ₹799 is the best pick for most people. On 21 November 2026 it had five sockets, four USB ports, a 2 m cord, a 1 year warranty and 4.3 stars from 6,004 ratings on Amazon.in. The GM Cuba 3341 at ₹646 is the pick if you want a USB-C port.

### Can I fast charge my phone from an extension board USB port?
Usually not. Zebronics lists 5V 1A (5W) on its Power BAR ports, and Honeywell and Portronics list about 10W shared across all ports. Apple says iPhone 15 and later need an 18W or greater USB-C PD adapter to fast charge. Use a proper charger plugged into a socket for fast charging.

### Can I run a room heater or iron on an extension board?
It is not a good idea. A 2,000W heater draws close to 9A, while most of these boards use a 6A plug, which is about 1,440W at 240V. Plug heaters, irons, geysers and induction cooktops straight into a 16A wall socket.

### Are universal sockets safe in India?
BIS lists multistandard and 5 pin universal sockets among varieties not covered by IS 1293 that cannot be manufactured or sold. Almost all USB boards on Amazon.in still use them. If you buy one, check for an ISI or BIS marked plug and cord, safety shutters, and a firm fit for each plug.

### Does a spike guard protect against power cuts?
No. A spike guard or surge protection board only tries to limit voltage spikes. It does not keep devices running during a power cut. For that you need a UPS.
