## Where each one wins

**Price and track record.** The NC3300 is ₹205 cheaper and has about 3,400 more ratings, with the same 4.0 average. Its country of origin is listed as India.

**Warranty.** Lapcare states 12 months plainly. The Zebronics listing contradicts itself, with "1 year carry in to service center" in the bullets and "NO WARRANTY" in the technical details, so keep your invoice.

**Fit and features.** The Chillmate names a 15.6 inch limit, has five height steps and a removable phone stand. The NC3300 does not state a maximum laptop size, so check its 36.8 x 26 cm top against your laptop.

Neither listing has a fan speed control. Lapcare claims 15 dB and "35% faster heat reduction" without a test method, so do not read too much into those numbers.
