## The distinctive part: the watt rating vs the USB port

### What a USB port can actually supply

All four lights run on USB power at 5 volts. The USB Implementers Forum's own rule is that a standard USB 2.0 port supplies up to 500 mA, and a standard USB 3.0 port up to 900 mA. At 5 V that works out to 2.5 W and 4.5 W. A USB-C port without fast charging tops out at 15 W (5 V at 3 A), according to Texas Instruments' USB-C guide.

So a "10 W" ring light plugged into an old laptop's USB-A port cannot draw 10 W. It either runs dimmer or the port cuts out.

### Digitek's own numbers do not add up

Digitek advertises the DRL-14 as a "30 Watt ring light" on its website, and the Amazon listing repeats "30 Watt output power". But the DRL-14 specification at Digitek's authorised dealer Sharp Imaging lists the input as **DC 5V / 1A**, and the manual for the closely related DRL-14C lists "Input: DC5V/1A, Output Power: 30W". Five volts at one amp is 5 W. A device cannot put out six times the power it takes in, so read "30 W" as a marketing figure, not what the light can draw. The same manual gives a more useful number, 2700 lumens, and warns against running the light for more than 6 hours at a stretch.

### What this means for you

1. **Use a wall adapter, not a laptop port.** A 5 V, 2 A phone charger is the sensible match. The Digitek DRL-14 includes an AC adapter, according to its Amazon title. The DRL-14C manual warns that the adapter should not exceed 2 A, or the cable can run hot.
2. **Compare lumens, not watts.** Only Digitek publishes a lumen figure (2700 lm, DRL-14C manual). Nobody else in this list does.
3. **Power banks work.** All four listings mention USB power, so a power bank runs them outdoors. See our [power bank under ₹1,500 guide]({{ "/best-power-bank-under-1500-in-india/" | relative_url }}).

### Which is the best ring light under Rs 1000 in India?

### Is a 30 W ring light brighter than a 10 W one?
Not necessarily. USB ring lights run at 5 V, and Digitek's own spec lists the DRL-14 input as 5 V at 1 A, which is 5 W, even though it is sold as 30 W. Compare lumens where published, and power the light from a proper wall adapter.

### Can I power a ring light from my laptop?
Yes, but a standard USB 2.0 port supplies only 2.5 W and a USB 3.0 port 4.5 W under USB-IF rules, so the light may not reach full brightness. A 5 V, 2 A wall adapter or a power bank is better.

### Which colour temperature is best for video calls?
Daylight, around 5000K to 5600K, looks neutral on most webcams. Warm settings near 3000K suit evening rooms with yellow bulbs. All four lights here have warm, natural and white modes.

### What size ring light do I need?
8 inch for close up selfies and makeup, 10 inch for face and shoulders on calls and reels, and 12 to 14 inch for softer light on half body shots.
