## Do this check first: can your laptop's USB-C port output video?

The HDMI socket on any hub only works if your laptop's USB-C port supports DisplayPort Alt Mode or is a Thunderbolt port.

VESA, the body that publishes the DisplayPort standard, says that if you plug a display into a USB-C port that does not support DisplayPort over USB-C, "no video data will be received by the display". Dell's support note says the same: if the USB-C port only supports data or power delivery, no video is seen. UGREEN's own listing adds that "the C port of the host device needs to support video output."

How to check in two minutes:

1. Look next to the port for a small DP (DisplayPort) logo or a lightning bolt (Thunderbolt). VESA says ports with DisplayPort over USB-C normally carry the DP logo.
2. If the port is unmarked, search your laptop model's spec sheet for "DisplayPort", "DP Alt Mode" or "video output" on USB-C.
3. If the port is data only, an HDMI hub still gives you USB ports but no screen, so the cheaper Zebronics TA210U makes more sense.

## How to choose a USB-C hub

### HDMI: 30Hz is fine for work, not for gaming

All four HDMI hubs here list 4K at 30Hz. That is fine for spreadsheets, documents, presentations and most video. For smoother scrolling or gaming on a 4K screen you need a 4K@60Hz hub, which on the same search page meant paying more, such as a UGREEN model with 4K@60Hz HDMI listed at ₹2,543.

### Charging: match the PD rating to your charger

Pass-through charging means you plug your laptop charger into the hub and the hub feeds the laptop. The hub keeps some power for itself, as UGREEN states with its 5W reservation. If your laptop came with a 65W charger, a 60W PD hub like the Mport 52 will still charge it, just a little more slowly under load. If it came with a 90W or 100W charger, pick a 100W PD hub. None of these hubs include a charger.

### Data speed: count the 5Gbps ports, not the total ports

USB-IF defines USB 3.2 at 5Gbps, 10Gbps and 20Gbps, and the older USB 2.0 tops out at 480 Mbps. Every hub here has only one fast USB-A port. Keep it for drives; USB 2.0 is plenty for a keyboard, mouse or webcam.

### Why does my USB-C hub's HDMI port show no picture?
Usually because the laptop's USB-C port does not support DisplayPort Alt Mode. VESA says a display connected to a USB-C port without DisplayPort over USB-C will receive no video. Check for a DP or Thunderbolt logo next to the port or your laptop's spec sheet.

### Can I charge my laptop through a USB-C hub?
Yes, if the hub has a PD input and you connect your own USB-C charger to it. The UGREEN Revodok passes through up to 95W of its 100W input, the Portronics C-Konnect lists 100W and the Portronics Mport 52 lists 60W. The Zebronics TA210U has no charging input.

### Are all USB ports on a hub equally fast?
No. On every hub in this guide only one USB-A port is a 5Gbps USB 3.0 port. The rest are slower USB 2.0 ports, which top out at 480 Mbps and suit a keyboard, mouse or webcam.

### Do cheap USB-C hubs support 4K at 60Hz?
None of the HDMI hubs in this guide do. All four list 4K at 30Hz, which suits office work and video. A 4K@60Hz model from UGREEN was listed at ₹2,543 on the same search page.
