## How to choose TWS earbuds under ₹2,000

### 1. Decide whether you need ANC

ANC is the single biggest feature jump in this budget. If you spend more than an hour a day in traffic, a train or an office with chatter, pick the realme T310 or T200x. If you mostly listen at home, the OnePlus gives you better battery and a higher rating instead.

### 2. Treat battery claims as a ceiling

Every brand measures "total playback" at moderate volume with ANC off. Expect 20% to 30% less with ANC on and the volume up. Even then, every pair here lasts several days of normal use between case charges.

### 3. Trust the number of ratings, not just the stars

A 4.3 from 52,000 buyers is far more reliable than a 4.5 from 300. Budget earbud listings are often merged across colours and older versions, so read a few recent one-star reviews before you buy.

### 4. Ignore the MRP

Most budget earbuds show an MRP three to five times the selling price. That number is not a useful guide to value. Compare the actual selling prices in the table above.

### 5. Check the codec and latency if you game

If you play mobile games, look for a low-latency or game mode. The realme T200x lists 45 ms, which is one of the lowest figures at this price.

### What is the difference between ANC and ENC?
ANC reduces the outside noise you hear. ENC only reduces background noise picked up by the microphone, so the person on the other end of a call hears you more clearly.

Most last one to two years of daily use before battery life drops noticeably. Buy from a brand with a service network and keep your invoice for warranty claims.
