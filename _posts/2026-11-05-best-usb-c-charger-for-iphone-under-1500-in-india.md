## How to choose a USB-C charger for iPhone under ₹1,500

### 1. Match the cable to your iPhone

iPhone 15, 16 and newer use USB-C. Older iPhones use Lightning. A USB-C wall adapter alone will not charge a Lightning iPhone without the right cable. If the listing does not include a cable, budget another ₹300 to ₹800 for a good MFi or USB-C cable.

### 2. 20W PD is the number that matters

Apple's fast-charge figure for recent iPhones is built around a 20W USB-C PD adapter. A 30W or 45W brick will not charge an iPhone much faster; it only helps if you also charge tablets or two devices.

### 3. Prefer ratings volume over a perfect star score

A 4.5 from 12,000 buyers (Apple) or a 4.0 from 14,000 buyers (APFEN) tells you more than a 4.7 from a few hundred. Skim recent one-star reviews for heat, early failure and fake-product complaints.

### 4. Check BIS, MFi and warranty, then buy fulfilled

BIS matters for wall adapters in India. MFi matters mainly for Lightning cables. Prefer Amazon Fulfilled listings. Six months is common at the low end; one year (Apple, Honeywell, Anker) is worth paying a bit more for daily use.

## FAQ

### What is the best USB-C charger for iPhone under ₹1,500 in India?
For most people who want the official product, the Apple 20W USB-C Power Adapter at ₹1,499. If you want an adapter and cable together for less, the APFEN 20W MFi kit at about ₹472 is the better value.

### Is a 20W charger enough for iPhone 15 and iPhone 16?
Yes. Apple rates fast charging for recent iPhones around a 20W USB-C PD adapter. Higher-watt bricks do not speed up a single iPhone in a useful way.

### Do I need an MFi charger for iPhone?
For the wall adapter, USB-C Power Delivery matters more than MFi. MFi certification matters mainly for Lightning cables used with iPhone 14 and earlier.

### Can I use an Android USB-C charger on an iPhone?
Yes, if it is a proper USB-C PD charger at about 20W. A charger that only does proprietary Android fast-charge protocols may fall back to slow 5W-class charging.

### Does the Apple 20W adapter include a cable?
No. The Amazon listing states the charging cable is sold separately. Pair it with USB-C to USB-C for iPhone 15 and later, or USB-C to Lightning for older models.
