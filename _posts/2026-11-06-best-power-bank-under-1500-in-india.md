## How to choose a power bank under ₹1,500

### Capacity: 10000mAh or 20000mAh

A 10000mAh bank is pocket sized and covers about one phone charge plus change. A 20000mAh bank is heavier but covers more days between charges. Capacity on the label is rated at the battery cell, so real usable capacity is always lower than the number printed.

### Output wattage

Most picks here say 22.5W. That is enough for fast charging most phones and earbuds. Do not expect to power a laptop from any of these. The Portronics Ignis 65 is the kind of model you look at for laptops, but it costs ₹1,999.

### Built-in cable or ports only

A built-in cable is convenient. A power bank with only ports gives you more choice of cable. If you choose the second type, budget for a decent Type-C cable.

### Ratings count matters more than the star score

A 4.1 from 1,740 buyers (Xiaomi) tells you more than a 4.2 from 48 buyers (EVM). Read recent one-star reviews for swelling, heat and early failure complaints. If a power bank ever swells, stop using it.

### Warranty and returns

Warranty terms were not consistently stated in the listing titles, with EVM's 2 year warranty the clear exception. Check the warranty line on the listing before ordering and keep the invoice.
