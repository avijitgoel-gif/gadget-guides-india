## The three numbers to check before you buy

### 1. Load rating: a phone is light, a phone plus a light is not

Apple lists the iPhone 16 Pro Max at 227 g, so even SYVO's 1 pound (about 450 g) rating covers a big phone with a case. The problem starts when you add a ring light, a microphone or a small camera. If you plan to grow into that, the Amazon Basics 134 cm (3 kg), Digitek DTR-420LW (3 kg) or Osaka OS 550 (2.5 kg) give you far more margin for the same money. Kreo and CIRYCASE do not state a load figure at all, though Kreo says the Stayble works with most ring lights.

### 2. Clamp width: measure your phone with its case

SYVO says its adapter fits phones up to a maximum stretch width of 3.4 inches (about 86 mm). Osaka says its clip adjusts between 3.3 and 4 inches.

Apple lists the iPhone 16 Pro Max at 3.06 inches (77.6 mm) wide. That leaves roughly 8 mm in the SYVO clip for a case on a very large phone. A thick rugged case could be a squeeze, so measure first.

### 3. Warranty: 10 days to 400 days

This was the biggest spread we saw: Kreo 400 days; both Amazon Basics models and CIRYCASE 1 year; Osaka 3 months for manufacturing defects only; both Digiteks 1 month; SYVO just "1" with no unit; and the Tygot stand 10 days replacement. Leg locks and clips are the parts that break, so a longer warranty is worth real money.

## FAQ

### Which is the best mobile tripod under Rs 1,000 in India?
The SYVO WT 3130 at ₹899 for most people. It was #1 in Complete Tripod Units on Amazon.in on 25 November 2026 with 4.2 stars from 36,140 ratings. If you want a longer warranty and more height, the Kreo Stayble at ₹899 reaches 160 cm and carries a 400 day warranty.

### How much weight can a cheap phone tripod hold?
It varies a lot. The SYVO WT 3130 lists 1 pound, the Osaka OS 550 lists 2.5 kg, and the Amazon Basics 134 cm and Digitek DTR-420LW list 3 kg. A large phone like the iPhone 16 Pro Max weighs 227 g, so all of them hold a phone. Load matters once you add a ring light or a camera.

### Will my big phone fit the tripod clip?
Check the clamp range. SYVO says its holder stretches to 3.4 inches and Osaka says its clip covers 3.3 to 4 inches. The iPhone 16 Pro Max is 3.06 inches wide without a case, so measure your phone with its case before buying.

### Can I use a phone tripod with a camera?
Yes, if it has a standard 1/4 inch screw. ISO 1222 sets 1/4-20 UNC as the tripod thread used by most consumer cameras. The Amazon Basics 134 cm tripod lists a quick release plate with a 1/4 inch screw and a 3 kg load.
