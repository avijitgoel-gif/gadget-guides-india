## The lost-receiver trap

Most cheap combos are paired at the factory to one small USB receiver. Lose it, or leave it in an old laptop, and both pieces stop working.

Logitech's support pages are clear about this for two of our picks. The MK215 keyboard and mouse can only use receiver C-U0011, and the MK240's only approved receiver is C-U0006. Neither is a Unifying receiver, so a Unifying dongle from another Logitech mouse will not work. Logitech points buyers to a spare-parts page to check whether a replacement is sold in their region.

Three habits avoid the problem:

1. **Leave the receiver in a rear USB port** on a desktop, or in the same laptop port, instead of carrying it around.
2. **Note the receiver model number** printed on it when you unbox the combo.
3. **If you switch between laptops often**, consider a Bluetooth keyboard and mouse instead, which pair directly without a dongle.
