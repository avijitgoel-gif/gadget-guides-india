## The rule most stand buyers miss: add a separate keyboard

A laptop's screen and keyboard are fixed together. Raise the screen and the keyboard rises too. The US Occupational Safety and Health Administration's purchasing guide says to "provide an external keyboard and input device for laptops and tablets", because laptop keyboards are generally not suitable for prolonged typing.

So the full setup is:

1. **Stand under the laptop** to lift the screen.
2. **External keyboard and mouse** on the desk at about elbow height. Our [wireless keyboard and mouse combos under ₹1,500]({{ "/best-wireless-keyboard-mouse-combo-under-1500-in-india/" | relative_url }}) guide covers sets from about ₹1,020, and a [wireless mouse under ₹1,000]({{ "/best-wireless-mouse-under-1000-in-india/" | relative_url }}) is enough if you only want to stop using the trackpad.
3. **Chair adjusted** so your feet are supported.

If you only type for a few minutes at a time, a low angle setting without an external keyboard is fine. If you work on the laptop for hours, budget for both. A ₹240 stand plus a ₹1,020 combo still costs less than most single ergonomic gadgets.

## How high should the screen be?

OSHA's guidance on monitors says the top of the screen should be at or slightly below eye level, and the centre of the screen should normally sit 15 to 20 degrees below horizontal eye level. It suggests a viewing distance of 20 to 40 inches, about 50 to 100 cm, and warns that a screen set too high makes you tilt your head back, which fatigues the neck.

In practice, sit normally, look straight ahead, and set the stand so your eyes land near the top edge of the screen. If the highest setting still leaves the screen too low, you need a taller riser or an external monitor, not a different brand of the same stand. Bifocal wearers may need the screen lower than this, as OSHA notes.

### Material: plastic or aluminium

Plastic stands like the STRIFF are cheap and heavy enough to sit firmly on a desk. Aluminium stands like the Dyazo, Portronics My Buddy K and Amazon Basics weigh 200 to 270 grams per their listings and fold small enough for a laptop bag.

### Laptop size

Check the largest size each stand lists. The Dyazo and Amazon Basics stop at 15.6 inches. The Portronics My Buddy K, Zebronics NS2000 Plus and My Buddy K9 list 17 inches.

### Warranty

Warranties range from "No Warranty" on the Zebronics to 1 year on the Dyazo and My Buddy K9. On a ₹300 product this matters mostly for hinges that loosen over time.

Every listing claims better airflow. Open frames do leave more space under the laptop than a flat desk, but none of the listings give test data, so treat cooling as a side benefit.
